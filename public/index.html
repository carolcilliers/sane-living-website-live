Hiding in plain sight

Behind the purple doors on Matilija Street

For years, people walked right past without knowing what was inside: a soundproof auditorium with stained glass, a real stage and a full sound and projection system.

Founded by Aubrey Balkind as the Sane Living Center, it has quietly hosted talks, concerts, retreats, film nights and celebrations. Now the doors are open wider, for the whole town and everyone visiting it.
