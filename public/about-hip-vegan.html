Dining Next Door

A Favorite Ojai Café, Steps From Our Door

Your guests don’t have to go far for a great meal.

Hip Vegan shares our corner of downtown Ojai. Its all-organic, plant-based menu draws on flavors from the owners’ travels, from Japanese-inspired bowls to tempeh burgers.

Inside, there’s a bright, airy dining room. Outside, a shady, dog-friendly patio makes it easy to linger between sessions or after an event.
