The Spaces

Choose Your Space

Four rooms with four different moods. Use one, or put them together for the whole day.

01

The Auditorium

Seats 110 · 225 standing

The heart of the building: a soundproof room with stained glass, a real stage and a full sound and projection system. Set it in rows for a talk, in a circle for a workshop, with tables for a reception or wide open for a dance floor.

  • Stage
  • Projector & Screen
  • Sound System
  • Theater Lighting
  • Soundproof Walls
  • Restrooms
02

The Conference Room

Meetings · Breakouts · Getting ready

A calm, separate room with a long wooden table, a whiteboard and natural light. Use it for meetings and breakout sessions, or as a private room to get ready on a wedding day.

  • Long Table
  • Whiteboard
  • Natural Light
  • Restrooms
03

The Annex

Multi-use flex space

A bright, flexible room with white walls, part hardwood floor and part carpet, right next to the Secret Garden. Set it up for a class, a breakout, a quiet lounge or a staging area for your event.

  • Hardwood & Carpet
  • White Walls
  • Next to the Secret Garden
  • Restrooms
04

The Secret Garden

Outdoors · Ceremonies · Dinners

A hidden garden with an olive tree, warm gravel, a white fence and string lights overhead. Set long tables for a dinner, an arch for a ceremony, or keep it open for a cocktail hour under the lights.

  • Olive Tree
  • String Lights
  • Open Air
  • Restrooms
