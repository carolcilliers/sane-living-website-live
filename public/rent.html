The Auditorium

Seats 110 · 200 standing

The heart of the building: a soundproof room with stained glass, a real stage and a full sound and projection system. Set it in rows for a talk, in a circle for a workshop, with tables for a screening or wide open for a dance floor. Made for making noise: the walls keep the music, laughter, cheers and happy tears inside, long after dark.
