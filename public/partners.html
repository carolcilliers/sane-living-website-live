Bringing your own vendor?

Outside vendors are welcome at Sane Living. We simply ask for proof of insurance (especially caterers and anyone serving alcohol) and that vendors follow our load-in, cleanup, and house rules. We are a preferred-vendor venue — we refer our partners because we trust them, never for referral fees.
