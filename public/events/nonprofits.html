The Sane Community Project

Building a Saner Community

A non-profit initiative that opens the doors of the Sane Living Center to everyone.

The Sane Community Project offers free and subsidized events that nourish mind, body and community. Rooted in the Sane Living philosophy of balancing Bio, Ego and Algo, it’s built on a simple belief: reconnecting with each other and with nature is the most hopeful path forward.

Our programming brings together wellness practitioners, artists, educators and neighbors to share knowledge, build relationships and strengthen the fabric of our community.

Accessible

Community events are free or offered at a reduced cost, so finances are never a barrier.

Inclusive

Open to all of Ojai and the greater Ventura County. No membership required.

Regenerative

Programs inspired by nature’s self-healing, for healthier minds, bodies and bonds.

“Reconnecting to nature can be our grounding touchstone to bring Bio, Ego and Algo into a healthier balance.”

Aubrey Balkind, Founder
