Inspiration

Great ideas from reliable sources — thought leaders and innovators from around the world.

Yuval Noah Harari

Israeli historian and author of Sapiens, Homo Deus, and 21 Lessons for the 21st Century. Harari writes about the "cognitive revolution" and examines free will, consciousness, intelligence, and happiness. He has said, "Homo sapiens as we know them will disappear in a century or so."

David Attenborough

Celebrated English broadcaster and natural historian whose career spans seven decades. His iconic series — Life on Earth, The Blue Planet, Planet Earth — have defined wildlife filmmaking and inspired generations to engage with environmental issues.

Sam Harris

American author, philosopher, and neuroscientist. Host of the Making Sense podcast and creator of the Waking Up meditation app. His work spans rationality, ethics, free will, neuroscience, meditation, and artificial intelligence.

Elon Musk

Business magnate, engineer, and founder of SpaceX, Tesla, The Boring Company, and Neuralink. Co-founder of OpenAI. His work spans electric vehicles, space exploration, brain-computer interfaces, and sustainable energy.
