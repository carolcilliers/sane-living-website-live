The EVO 3 Sculpture

Standing over 8 feet tall on East Matilija Street in Ojai, California, the EVO3 Sculpture offers viewers the story of humanity — from its biological root through its egocentric development to its algorithmically-defined future — in the form of a giant metallic question mark with a drum integrated into its base.

Created by Sane Living founder Aubrey Balkind and built by artist Ray Cerino, the EVO 3 sculpture was welcomed by the Ojai City Council in 2018.
