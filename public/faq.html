Booking & Rates

How do I check if my date is available?

Open the calendar on the Rent page. Purple dates are already booked. Then send us your date, guest count and plans through the request form, and the team will follow up to confirm the details and your rate.

How much does it cost to rent the space?

Rates depend on the day and the time block: Day (8am–5pm), Night (5pm–12am) or Day & Night. 2026 rates start at $400, and new 2027 rates begin January 1, 2027. See the full table on Rates & Availability.

What are your hours?

Events can run from 8am to 2am. Our office is open 9am to 5pm for calls and questions.

Can I book just a few hours?

Yes. Book the morning, the evening or the whole day. A meeting at 8am and a reception at 7pm can share the same room on the same day.

Can I see the space before I book?

Please do. The best way to picture your event is to stand in the room. Mention a walk-through when you get in touch and we’ll find a time.

The Spaces

How many guests can you hold?

The Auditorium seats 110, theater-style or at tables, and holds 225 standing.

Which spaces can I rent?

Four, all at one location: the Auditorium, the Conference Room, the Annex and the Secret Garden. Start with the Auditorium and add the others as you need them. See all four spaces.

What equipment is included?

The Auditorium has a full sound system, a projector and screen, theater lighting and a portable stage we set up on request. The Conference Room has a large wooden table, a 56″ wall-mounted monitor and a whiteboard.

Do you provide tables and chairs?

Yes, chair and table rentals are available. Tell us your guest count and layout, whether that’s theater-style, a circle, dinner tables or an open dance floor, and we’ll set the room.

Food, Drink & Music

Can I bring my own caterer?

Yes. Outside caterers are welcome, and you can even have a food truck set up in our private parking lot. Caterers need to provide proof of insurance and follow the venue’s load-in, cleanup and house rules.

Can we serve alcohol?

Yes. You’re welcome to provide and serve your own alcohol, as long as alcohol service is covered under your event’s Certificate of Insurance (COI).

How late can the music go?

Outdoor sound in the Secret Garden wraps up by 10pm. The Auditorium is built for celebration, with sound-insulated walls, so the music and dancing can carry on inside until 2am. Amplified music stays inside the Auditorium.

Weddings

Do you host weddings?

Yes. Say your vows beneath the stained glass, have dinner under the string lights in the Secret Garden, then dance in the Auditorium, all at one location. See wedding photos and highlights.

How is wedding pricing different?

Weddings are quoted separately from our standard rates, so each quote fits your guest count, timeline and vision. Call (805) 646-6000 to start the conversation.

Is there a room to get ready in?

Yes. The Annex works as a private bridal room, just down the hall from the Auditorium and next to the Secret Garden.

Where can our guests stay?

Downtown hotels are a short walk away, so guests can walk to the ceremony and back without shuttles. For the wedding party, our partner Cielo Farm is a 20+ acre orchard retreat in the hills that sleeps up to 15.

Getting Here

Where are you?

Behind the purple doors at 316 E Matilija St, Ojai, CA 93023, a short walk from the Ojai Playhouse, Libbey Park, hotels and restaurants.

Is there parking?

Yes. We have a private parking lot, with street parking nearby and valet available for larger events.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The venue is on the ground floor, with accessible restrooms and an accessible parking spot.
