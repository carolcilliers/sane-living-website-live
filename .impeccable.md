## Design Context

### Users
Sane Living team members managing events, venue photos, pricing, policies and website pages. Many are not technical. Work happens on desktop and phones; edits must remain recoverable and Save, Preview and Publish must have clear meanings.

### Brand personality
Calm, welcoming, precise. Confidence and ease matter more than decoration.

### Aesthetic direction
Paul requested clean, smooth Apple-like controls and a Framer-like editor; readable fields and restrained buttons. Keep Sane Living's real venue imagery and purple brand identity. The editor defaults to dark with an available light theme. Use warm neutral surfaces, a single restrained plum accent, consistent hairlines, quieter cards and clear type hierarchy. Existing Nunito branding is retained; no new font dependency.

### Design principles
1. Make the task and the next action clear; reserve emphasis for saving/publishing and selection.
2. Use spacing and typography for grouping; avoid cards nested inside cards, strong gradients and decorative shadows.
3. Make fields, status feedback and touch controls easy to read and operate.
4. Keep the actual website canvas faithful to published content; workspace styling stays outside it.
5. Protect staff work: recovery keeps originals, publication remains explicit, and visual refinements must pass the existing editing tests.
